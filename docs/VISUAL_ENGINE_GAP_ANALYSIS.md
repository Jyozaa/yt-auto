# Visual engine gap analysis

Baseline is YT-auto `f2f670e`. Source is [Mr omi’s MIT bridge template](https://github.com/mromidev/claude-animated-shorts/tree/e3b817b6afc6cd413466d43f11ba31209e034bbf/templates/bridge) at `e3b817b6afc6cd413466d43f11ba31209e034bbf`. The bridge story is a development fixture. The production contract continues to use JSON and narration word timings.

The seven reports informed the checks above. Character and outline studies separate silhouette, facial colour and edge weight. Motion measures 18–40-frame glides and short alternating impact kicks. Depth and texture reports separate painted seams from world ink, call for selective close-shot blur and tie fog to the sky. Lighting and grade target daylight median luma around 0.57 and non-ink fifth-percentile luma around 0.23. These are reference measurements, with shot-specific lighting and subject differences expected. The source still, merge, side-by-side and QA scripts informed the comparison workflow. Third-party studies under `references/frames` and `references/sheets` are excluded from output.

The template references 26 Kenney GLB names under `public/bridge/kenney` and ships none. Source geometry already replaces the principal trees, bushes, hedges and banners. The kit inventory and exact-match or fallback decisions are recorded in the attribution. The source narration WAV and word track are absent. The development fixture uses the authored frame table and renders silently.
